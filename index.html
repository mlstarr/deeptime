How to play
  • Cells are real. Each dot eats from its patch, pays upkeep, divides, and dies. Every division can mutate. Most mutants are worse and vanish; a few are better and sweep.
  • Drafts. Time stops between epochs. Seed one mutation into each of your species and place it on the map. Selection and luck decide whether it spreads. “Leads to” shows what a gene unlocks.
  • Sway refills quickly to 40 and slowly to 60. Spend it on refuges, currents, isolation, stress bursts, and opportunities.
  • Opportunities pop up mid-epoch when something happens: a promising mutant, predators, new habitat, genes on offer. Time slows while you decide.
  • Events are forecast. Ten seconds before one hits, you learn which it is. Refuges cost less before the warning.
  • Keys: Space pauses, 1–4 set speed.
